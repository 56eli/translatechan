## 4. One behaviour change, disclosed

The check *"an option the register does not record is reported, not silently merged"* typed
`--doc wumenguan` against the live register and relied on that register happening **not** to record
`--doc`. The 2026-09-22 P1 Biyanlu register records every replayable flag in
`collate_corpus.REPLAYABLE_FLAGS`, including `doc`, so under a simple re-pin the line would have
tested a *conflict* rather than an *unrecorded option* — the assertion could never fire again.

The premise is now constructed rather than borrowed: the test copies the register into a sandbox,
strips the `doc` key, and types `--doc` at the stripped copy. It also asserts that the live register
does record `doc`, so the setup cannot silently rot. The assertion itself is unchanged in substance
and remains fail-closed.

No other check changed behaviour. Nothing was deleted, relaxed, skipped, or made conditional.

## 8. Standing rule adopted from this incident

A PR that changes the document count, the complete-document set, or the authoritative register must
re-pin all three checkers **in the same PR**. Recorded in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` §3. The corpus data
and the pins that describe it ship together, or the gate is lying.
