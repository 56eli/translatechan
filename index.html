🛡️ Sandboxed Sessioned Execution
All AI translation drafts and CBETA canonical ingestion in TranslateChan are executed by sessioned Arena AI Agents operating in isolated sandboxes. The agents ingest Classical Chinese texts, segment dialogues, enforce lexicon consistency, and commit structured JSON directly to Git.

⚖️ Multi-Register Translation Pipeline
1. Literal & Philological Register
Preserves Tang/Song vernacular Chinese particles (底, 甚麼, 這箇, 阿誰) and strict syntax.
2. Philosophical & Hermeneutic Register
Expands non-dual ontology, Mind-Only, Emptiness (śūnyatā), and dialectical structures.
3. Poetic & Zen Cadence Register
Captures the sharp, brisk, enigmatic cadence of Chan masters (Red Pine & Cleary style).
